Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

FlavorMatch is an offline matching and cooking game provided by PanYue. This policy explains how the Android app (com.panyue.flavormatch) handles information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores game information in its private storage so you can continue playing. This includes level progress and scores, coins and lives, ingredients and prepared dishes, recipe and customer unlocks, cooking progress and timers, daily tasks, tutorial progress, and sound preferences. The app processes this information locally to provide gameplay, save progress, and schedule reminders.

Data collection and sharing

The current version does not require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or send your gameplay data to a server operated by us. We do not collect your contacts, precise location, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifier, or payment details through the app. We do not sell or share your gameplay data with advertisers.

Optional notifications

On supported Android versions, the app may request notification permission to show local reminders when cooking finishes or daily tasks refresh. These reminders are scheduled on your device rather than sent from a developer-operated server. You can decline this permission and continue playing, or turn notifications off in Android Settings.

Android backup and platform services

Android may back up and restore app data depending on your device, account, and backup settings. Backup and device-transfer services are provided by Google or your device provider under their own policies. We do not operate these services or receive a copy of your device backup. Your use of Google Play and Android is also subject to the relevant platform policies.

Retention and deletion

Game information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. These actions remove local game progress. A platform backup may still contain a copy, and restoring a backup may restore game progress. Manage or delete such backups using your device or backup-provider settings. There is no FlavorMatch account to delete, and we do not hold a server-side copy of your gameplay save.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal requirements. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to those requirements.

Security

The app uses Android's app-private storage and the device's access controls for local game information. You can help protect your information by securing your device and managing its backup and notification settings.

Children

The app does not ask players to provide personal information and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support message, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Online policy page

If you open the online version of this policy, the hosting provider may process technical information, such as your IP address, to deliver the page under its own policies. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, or data-collection forms to the policy page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. We will update the app's policy and applicable Google Play disclosures to reflect material changes.
